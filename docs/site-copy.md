# Splash page wording

The words on the splash page (`site/src/pages/index.astro`). Older versions are
kept here so they can be reused.

## Current (Ed, Nov 2026)

> A field journal that listens here & now

**Birdsongs.ai** (was "Birdsongs"; on trial)

Take it on a walk. It listens as you go. Trusty private local AI.
Every bird it hears is lovingly illustrated in the manner of the old
natural-history books: dated, placed on the map, along with the weather of
the day.

*As old as time. Each walk becomes an artwork, with it's own Index of Species.*

*Now I wonder how many you might collect?*

Earlier taglines: "A field journal that listens now", "Be here now", "I just spotted Kingfisher! :D" (the
notification's words, now with Northern Cardinal), "I wonder who has the fairest call of them all?".
